# Menopause Supplement Guide: Free Starter

An interactive introduction to dietary supplements for women in perimenopause, menopause and postmenopause. It explains what supplements can and cannot do in menopause, why the market is so confusing, and how to judge the evidence behind any claim.

The skill is evidence-based and informed by the personal experience of two real people, Dani and Kevin. They are not medical professionals, and the supplements they take are described as their own experience, not as recommendations.

## What is included

- Part 1: what supplements can and cannot do in menopause
- Six questions to ask about any supplement, and four plain evidence labels
- A short guided walk-through that ends with questions for your doctor or pharmacist
- Essential safety guidance, including when to see a clinician
- A short reference list

## How to use it

Install the plugin, start a new chat, and try one of these:

- "I'm in perimenopause and confused about supplements. Where do I begin?"
- "What can supplements actually do for menopause symptoms?"
- "How do I judge whether a supplement claim is backed by evidence?"

The guide will ask a few questions about you, then explain what applies to your situation.

## Data and privacy

This plugin is a set of written instructions only. It does not collect, store or send any data, and it does not connect to any outside service.

## Troubleshooting and support

If the guide does not respond as described, start a new chat and check that the plugin is enabled. For questions or problems, open an issue at https://github.com/Kev-Dani/menopause-supplement-starter/issues

## Please note

This is an AI-powered, self-guided skill. You are not communicating with Dani or Kevin, and it does not include direct access to them. It provides educational information only. It is not medical, pharmaceutical or dietetic advice, diagnosis or treatment. Ask your primary care physician or pharmacist before starting, stopping or changing any supplement or medicine. No results are guaranteed.
